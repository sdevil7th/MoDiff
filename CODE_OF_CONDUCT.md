# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

MoDiff welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and perspectives. Contributors, maintainers, and users should help make project spaces respectful, safe, and useful.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- Giving and receiving technical feedback with empathy and specificity
- Focusing criticism on code, documentation, behavior, and impact rather than the person
- Respecting different experience levels and asking clarifying questions in good faith
- Acknowledging mistakes, correcting them, and learning from them
- Protecting private information and following responsible vulnerability disclosure
- Accepting maintainer decisions about scope while remaining free to discuss alternatives constructively

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or demeaning comments
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberate misinformation
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Sharing credentials, private media, exploit details, or sensitive diagnostics in public project spaces
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, security-reporting interactions, and other spaces where someone represents the MoDiff project.

## Reporting And Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers whenever possible. If the concern is a security vulnerability, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). If no private channel is available, open a minimal public issue asking for private contact without including sensitive details.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participation when behavior threatens the safety or effectiveness of the project. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, intent, prior behavior, and the safety of affected people.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Retaliation for a good-faith report is itself a violation.
